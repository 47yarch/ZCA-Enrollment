Attendance Policy

Understanding our attendance rules helps maintain a consistent and effective learning environment for all students.

✅

Excused Absences

Students must submit a written explanation or medical certificate to justify absences. Excused absences include illness, family emergencies, and approved school activities.

❌

Unexcused Absences

Absences without valid justification are considered unexcused. Repeated unexcused absences may result in disciplinary actions.

🕐

Late Policy

Students arriving late must report to the office and obtain a late slip. Frequent tardiness may affect participation and grades.

🚪

Early Dismissal

Early dismissal requires a written request from a parent or guardian. Students must sign out at the office before leaving campus.
